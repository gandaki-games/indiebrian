Shootpunk

A fast-paced aerial survival roguelike featuring automated target firing, manual missile evasion, and modular ship upgrade progression.

Gameplay Core Loop

Players pilot a custom ship through increasing enemy waves. While primary weapons target incoming threats automatically, players must manually deploy limited flare charges to dodge tracking missiles, collect dropped salvage parts, and continuously upgrade performance during each run.

7-Day Sprint Roadmap

Day 1: Core Loop Lock

Player movement, auto-shooting, enemy spawning, salvage drops, and shop screen state loops.

Day 2: Enemy Roster

Implemented 4 distinct roles: Basic Fighter, Fast Interceptor, Heavy Shooter, and Missile Fighter.

Day 3: Progression & Parts

Scaling upgrade costs across Core and Combat parts (Speed, Armor, Health, Fire Rate, Flares).

Day 4: Signature Missile System

Visual missile proximity warnings paired with manual flare cooldown mechanics.

Day 5: Difficulty Progression

Time-based wave scaling and end-of-run survival summary statistics.

Day 6 & 7: VFX Polish & Windows Build

Explosion animations, hit audio feedback, UI balancing, bug fixes, and standalone executable generation.
